## Multi-head
model has several separate attention mechanisms ("heads") that each learn to focus on different aspects of the input. 

- ex make prediction of temperature and traffic

 each head is a reader with different focus
- ex:
	- head 1 focuses on grammar structure
	- head 2 focuses on coreferences ("it" refers to "the cat")
	- head 3 attends to semantic similarity ("fast" ~ "quick")


ex: "The animal didn't cross the ***street*** because it was too tired"
